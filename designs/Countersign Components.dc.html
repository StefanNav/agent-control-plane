Agent Control Plane · Lakeshore Health · Countersign, light mode first Product components

Ten components from the brief on Countersign. IBM Plex, a near-neutral grey, hairlines and radius 3/6. Indigo is selection and the primary action; teal means only review waiting. Status chips are outlined in their colour, rule tags are outlined in grey, labels are uppercase mono. Tables and boards run at 13 px; forms and signing stay comfortable at 16 px with 44 px fields. Dark mode repeats at the end.

What didn't hold once built
Two accents, each split per modeIndigo selects and acts; teal marks review. Both fall below 3:1 on the dark panel, so dark uses lighter values for marks and text and keeps deeper fills for buttons. Amber and red split the same way.
The ring is overloadedReview, monitor stale and Shadow are all circles: solid, dashed, half. At 12 px on a wall, solid vs dashed is the weakest pair in the set. Test it; if it fails, stale becomes a dashed square.
“Hard stop fired” has no severity ruleOne block means the system worked; three on one rule in a day means the agent keeps trying. I made the repeat Critical (it pages) and single blocks a log entry. That threshold needs writing down, per rule.
Two weights can't mark “new”600 vs 400 alone didn't separate new from claimed at a glance, so new exceptions also get a small ink dot. Still no colour.
Dashed was already takenA dashed border is the obvious “soft” style for instructions, but dashed already means no data. Instructions are soft through radius, an open fill and an Edit control; hard stops are solid ink, filled and locked.
Dialogs need a third layerTwo surface levels don't cover a modal: it needs a scrim. I kept the dialog flat, border and no shadow, on a light scrim.
12 px doesn't survive the wallRule tags and mono timestamps at 12 px work on a laptop, not across a room, even in dark. The wall view needs its own scale, as flagged earlier.
Sign and order cost two columns everywhereShowing them locked on every row is honest but makes 2 of 6 verb columns permanent noise. I kept them, greyed, with the policy named once in the header.
